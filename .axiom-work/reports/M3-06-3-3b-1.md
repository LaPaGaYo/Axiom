## Known limitations to retain

The check runs at the reconcile pass resolution (60 seconds), so a Task can overshoot by one pass of usage. TUI Attempts are neither measured nor stopped by the token rule. The Mission cap blocks admission only and never stops already-running Attempts. A capped Task's running Attempt is read twice per pass, once during supervision and again during metering. AgentSpec still shows the policy cap when the Task has none. Planner runs are not counted.
