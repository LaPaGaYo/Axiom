## Differences and limitations

- Frozen `supervision-policy.ts` remains untouched; proposal thresholds (20 and 0.9) and scan limit (100) live together in the adjacent `stall-proposal-policy.ts`.
- Empty evidence has throughSequence=0 (no counted reconciliation); unknown endings with no ending audit use the reconciliation context's now. A Submission with no verification runs is inconclusive/unknown under the brief's “some non-terminal run” pending predicate. These edge conventions are documented.
- Missing or mismatched bindings are logged as single-observation read failures and retained; identity is not fabricated.
- An outcome is final when written; later verification reruns do not revise it.
- Pending observations of a Mission that never resumes stay pending.
- The decision window restarts after every global decision; Mission overrides do not reset it. Dismissal starts after the proposal snapshot, retaining evidence accumulated while it was open.
- At most 100 oldest unreconciled observations are examined per pass; 100 permanently pending oldest rows can prevent newer rows from being examined.
- No UI is added; M3-09 owns it. No tokens-saved claim; M3-06-3-3 owns token accounting.
- CLI Agent write scope remains verification-time diff enforcement.
- Full acceptance cannot be claimed until the three existing-test expectation conflicts are resolved and the host verifier runs its Electron checks. This worktree has not been merged or promoted.
