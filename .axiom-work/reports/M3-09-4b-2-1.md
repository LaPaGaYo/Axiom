## Limitations and risks

- Context is built at Planner launch. A plan proposed by hand and rejected after the context is built, while launch is in flight or the Planner Attempt is running, does not reach that Attempt. Its later proposal is still accepted under the existing admission/proposal rules. Rejection does not automatically start a Planner.
- Older rejections fall out at the 4 KiB section limit. A Task list stops at 1 KiB of entries and separators, with the omitted count outside that list budget. Reasons stop at 500 UTF-16 code units and the existing quote byte cap, without a truncation marker. Complete reasons remain in their PlanRevisions and `plan.rejected` audits.
- Zero-width, bidirectional, U+2028 / U+2029 and C1 characters in reasons and titles are quoted but not stripped, matching other `quote` / `quoteLine` users; the broader DEF-12 ② character hygiene work is not added here.
- Only Task ids and titles of rejected plans are included. Objectives, paths, criteria, edges, milestones and warnings are not copied into this section.
- Rejections of plans for an earlier Goal are included and marked, not filtered out.
- The write guard uses built-in secret patterns only, not Mission-specific `verificationRules.secretPatterns`. Already stored reasons are neither scanned nor rewritten; their prompt rendering is redacted. Pattern detection is not a guarantee that arbitrary sensitive text is recognized.
- The Planner receives guidance rather than deterministic enforcement of the rejection reason; a person still reviews the next plan.
- CLI-agent write scope is a verification-time diff boundary rather than a runtime filesystem guarantee. This work was checked against the exact allowlist, including untracked files, and did not expand permissions.
