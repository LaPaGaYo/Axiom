## Concurrency and losing capture

Another authorized Control Plane path can settle or cancel the Task while capture awaits filesystem/Git/Artifact IO. The counted-settlement race test uses the existing settleAttemptWithoutSubmission at that boundary: its task.stage rows remain NEEDS_FIX(attempt_abandoned) then READY(retry), count remains 1, and this losing branch writes no failed/preserved audit or continuation effect. The test also passes on main, where that counted outcome is the original path.

The cancellation case invokes MissionController.cancelMission during capture. The Task is CANCELED; this branch writes no failed/preserved audit or refund. Reconciliation begins again at the top and the existing cancellation resource path may write attempt.abandoned with reason mission_canceled (and its release receipt), which is separate from the interrupted branch. Task cancellation or a terminal Mission similarly invalidates authority. A newer binding/dispatch or changed worker execution identity also invalidates the guard.

The generic guard also checks for a Submission. For this specific already-abandoned kernel state, a new ordinary Worker submission or exit settlement cannot legitimately win: resolveHostAttempt requires dispatched + ready/start_unknown and rechecks inside settlement; Worker mutation authority is revoked by the kernel. RecoveryService serializes its own overlapping reconcileAttempts calls. The race test exercises the durable-authority guard, not a claim that normal polling starts two concurrent captures. A lost capture can leave a published immutable ref or unattached Artifact; neither is adopted into an audit unless the winning settlement owns it. No second capture is scheduled by this branch.
