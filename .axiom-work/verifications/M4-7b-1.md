What this Task is: a person decides a waiting delivery Approval. Since M4-7a a Mission that becomes `READY_FOR_APPROVAL` has one `PENDING` delivery Approval bound to the hash of its completion report, and nobody could decide it. This candidate adds the two methods a person decides with, `axiom.approval.grant` and `axiom.approval.deny` (desktop only, durable mutations). A grant checks, in one write, that the Approval exists and is a delivery Approval, that it is `PENDING`, that its Mission is `READY_FOR_APPROVAL`, that its hash is the hash of the Mission's current ready record, and that its expiry has not passed; an expired request is closed as `EXPIRED` and requested again for the same report in a write that commits, and only then is the call refused. A denial records the reason and blocks the Mission with `delivery_denied`. The expiry clock of an Approval stops while its Mission is in the status `PAUSED` (D70 item 4, the Approval part): every move out of `PAUSED` moves the expiry of the Mission's open delivery Approvals forward by the time just spent there. A granted Mission shows a pending item `delivery_approved`. Nothing delivers, nothing consumes, no timer runs, no interface changes.
