- **A decision for you** (pending list, new item 13): may an Approval that was granted but not yet used be withdrawn or expire? The rules today say a granted Approval has one way out, being consumed by the delivery. I think two more ways out are clearer — withdrawn when the Mission is canceled or its report is replaced, expired when its time has passed — so that "granted" always means "usable now"; without them such a row stays "granted" for ever and only the check at delivery stops it (ARCHITECTURE §14 asks for that check of the hash and the expiry in any case). It needs one sentence of `AGENTS.md`, one line each of ARCHITECTURE and GLOSSARY, and a revision line in the decision log. It does not hold M4-7b up: until you say so, the code follows the rules as written, and the two moves can be added later as a small Task.
- **The pause clock.** D70 item 4 stands: the expiry of an Approval stops while the Mission is paused. Nothing acts on expiry yet; M4-7b will implement it.
- **Verifier's details, yours to overturn** (in the brief and the plan row): the Approval is bound to the hash of the completion report and to nothing else; 72 hours; no re-check of the report or the commits while a Mission waits — the grant will compare with the record, the delivery with git; a pending delivery request blocks neither admission nor the gate.
