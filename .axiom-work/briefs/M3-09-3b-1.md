# Task M3-09-3b-1 — Mission Home says since when each pending item has waited; what M3-09-3a left behind is removed (one read-model rule, renderer)

Planner brief, 2026-11-14 (verifier). M3-09-3 (Mission Home) was split in three; its second part, M3-09-3b, is split again here: this part gives every pending row its start time and clears the ground, and M3-09-3b-2 adds the quiet timed refresh on top. M3-09-3a showed no time because one kind of `since` moved with every admission round, and it kept a read and a prop nothing uses any more so that its red check could run. Both are settled here, before a timer starts re-reading the page every few seconds.

Read first: `AGENTS.md` (UI and Product Experience; Project Control Plane Boundary), `docs/STYLEGUIDE.md` (UX rule 4), `docs/GLOSSARY.md` §4.4a, `docs/reference/control-plane.md` (the paragraph on `since` in the M3-09-1 section, and "Mission 首页的待处理项（M3-09-3a）").

## Ground truth (verifier, 2026-11-14, main `5adb15ba89`; every fact here was read in the code, the tests or the specs at that commit)

1. **`since` today.** Every item of `axiom.mission.pending` carries `since`, a UTC ISO 8601 string taken from a persisted row or audit, never from the time of the read. For a Task item it comes from `taskSince` (`src/main/axiom/read-models/pending-task-items.ts`): the latest run's `updatedAt` when the wait names a run; for `no_eligible_provider` the **latest** `task.admission_refused` audit of the Task (the Task's `updatedAt` if there were none, which `admissionWait` does not let happen); for `upstream_canceled` the earliest cancellation among the upstream Tasks; otherwise the Task's stage audit (with the budget variants of `pending-since.ts`). `since` is also the fourth key of the view's order.
2. **Why the provider wait moves.** The scheduler writes one `task.admission_refused` audit for every refused Task in every round (`scheduler.ts`), and a round runs at boot for every executing Mission. `admissionWait` (`task-waiting-on.ts`) calls a refusal current when its `sequence` is above the sequence of the Task's stage audit (`ctx.stageAudit(task)`), and reports `no_eligible_provider` with `person: true` when that refusal names no account cooldown. So the wait is one state from the first such refusal on, while its `since` jumps forward at every round and at every start of the app.
3. **The store read.** `readModelAuditsStore(db).getLatestReadModelAudit` (`src/main/axiom/db/read-model-audits-store.ts`, 43 counted lines) is one SQL read — the newest audit of a Mission by `sequence`, filtered by object, kind, events and optionally the stage or status in `after` — and `AxiomStore` exposes it (`axiom-store.ts`, 254 counted lines of 300). There is no read for the oldest audit after a given sequence; `listAuditEvents` (`policy-audit-store.ts`) returns every audit of a Mission after a sequence, and that list grows by one refusal per waiting Task per round. No existing test expects a particular `since` for a provider wait (`mission-pending-boundaries.test.ts` checks that item's actions only).
4. **Mission Home.** `MissionPendingList.tsx` (102 counted lines) renders one row per item and does not read `since`. The missions components show recorded times as the ISO string inside `<time dateTime>` — the Attempts table, the submission, the verification runs and their dispositions; the one exception is the deadline on the Task page's waiting line (`TaskWaitingLine.tsx`), shown in local format. The sidecars of the last three parts guard against new date formatting, clock reads and timers in these components.
5. **The restart check.** `mission-exit-m1.spec.ts` compares the text of Mission Home and of every Task row before and after a restart. It makes three provider waits by disallowing every provider (`axiom.policy.update`, `routing.disallowedProviders`) before the plan is approved and the Mission started; the restart adds a refusal per Task.
6. **What M3-09-3a left.** `MissionHome.tsx` still declares the prop `pendingMessages` and reads nothing from it; `readMission` in `use-missions-page-controller.ts` still calls `axiomClient.listMessages({ missionId, pendingOnly: true })` on every refresh and returns the result as `pendingMessages`; `MissionsPageSurface.tsx` spreads the whole Mission detail into `MissionHome`. Tests: `MissionHome.test.tsx` passes the prop in four places and `MissionHomePending.test.tsx` in one; the controller test "loads Task detail and re-reads pending messages after an uncertain answer" expects that read after an answer whose response was lost. One existing expectation says the time is absent: `MissionHomePending.test.tsx`, in "lists the pending items of the Mission in the order the view returns them", checks that the item's `since` (`2026-11-13T12:00:00Z`) is not on the page. The sidecars `M3-09-3a.verify` and `M3-09-2b-4.verify` each hold two guards that keep the prop and the read; this brief's commit removes those four assertions, and the new sidecar asserts the opposite.
7. **The controller's size.** `use-missions-page-controller.ts` has 283 counted lines of 300. `readMission` is a module-private function of 48 lines at its top; the type of the controller's `detail` is derived from it.
8. **The other readers of a row.** `mission-home-pending.spec.ts` checks each row through its parts (kind, reason, the blocks line, `You can:`), not its whole text, and `MissionHomePending.test.tsx` compares whole text only for the `You can:` line — apart from the expectation named in 6, a new line in the row breaks neither.

## Outcome

Each pending row on Mission Home says since when it has waited, with the time the Control Plane recorded; for a Task that no provider may take, that is when the wait began, and it stays put through later rounds and restarts. The page no longer reads a list it does not show, and the controller has room for the timed refresh of the next part.

## Deliverables

1. **A provider wait is dated from its first refusal** (main process, read model). For an item whose Task waits on `no_eligible_provider`, `since` is the time of the oldest `task.admission_refused` audit of that Task whose sequence is above the Task's stage audit — the same boundary `admissionWait` uses; what it falls back to when there is none stays as it is. Later refusals of the same stage do not move it; a new stage starts a new wait. This needs one new read next to `getLatestReadModelAudit` (the oldest matching audit after a sequence, one row from SQL), exposed through `AxiomStore` the same way — not `listAuditEvents`, which would load every audit written since the stage began on each read of the view. Nothing else about the view changes: no field, no kind, no action, no other `since`; `waitingOn` is untouched.
2. **Mission Home shows it.** Each row of `MissionPendingList` gains one line: the label `Since` and the item's `since` exactly as the view returns it, inside `<time dateTime>` with the same string — what the Attempts table does with its times. The line sits before the `You can:` line. No formatting, no relative age ("5 min ago"), no clock: the same state reads the same at any moment.
3. **`pendingMessages` is gone.** The prop leaves `MissionHome`, the read and the field leave the Mission read, and the tests that pass or expect them are updated: the five places that pass the prop simply stop; the controller test of ground truth 6 expects the re-read of the pending view (`axiom.mission.pending`) instead, under a title that says so; and the expectation of ground truth 6 that the time is absent goes (that test keeps its title — the sidecar of M3-09-3a pins it). A refresh makes one call less.
4. **The Mission read moves out of the controller.** `readMission` becomes its own module in the missions folder, exported with the type the controller needs; the controller imports it. A pure move apart from deliverable 3: same calls, same order, same settled read of the pending view shared with the Task evidence. The controller ends at 250 lines or fewer.
5. **Catalogs.** One new key `missions.pendingSince` (`Since`), through `translate('missions.pendingSince', 'Since')`, in all seven catalogs (`en` and `zh` with real text; the others may carry the English text). No key is removed and no existing value changes.
6. **E2E** (host, verifier-run): a new spec `tests/e2e/mission-home-since.spec.ts`, `desktopCall` for setup and read-back, the two Mission actions through the page, responses parsed with the contract schemas, every wait an assertion with a timeout.
   - **Three provider waits keep their start.** A Mission on the fixture with every provider disallowed, its plan approved and the Mission started, as `mission-exit-m1` does: three Tasks are `READY` and refused. The pending view has three `task_blocked` items with reason `no_eligible_provider`; Mission Home lists three rows, and each row shows `Since` with a `time` element whose `datetime` and text are that item's `since`. Each `since` is the time of the first `task.admission_refused` audit of that Task (read back from the audit table, as ISO). Then `Pause Mission` and `Resume Mission` on the page; when every one of the three Tasks has a newer refusal audit whose recorded time is later than its first one's (the spec asserts this, so the check cannot pass on a `since` that follows the newest refusal; audit times have one-second precision) and the page has been refreshed, the view's three `since` values are the ones from before and the three rows read exactly as they did.
   - The spec attaches one screenshot of Mission Home with the three rows (`captureDetail` with the region name).
   - The report lists every asserted selector and field with its source.
7. **Docs.** `docs/reference/control-plane.md`: the `since` paragraph of the M3-09-1 section says how a provider wait is dated; the M3-09-3a section no longer says that no time is shown — a short section "Mission 首页的起始时间（M3-09-3b-1）" says what the row shows and from which field, that the string is not reformatted, and that `pendingMessages` and its read are gone. `docs/GLOSSARY.md` is not changed (its §4.4a sends the reader to `control-plane.md` for the sources of `since`).
