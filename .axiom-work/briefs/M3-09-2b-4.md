# Task M3-09-2b-4 — Task page: a quiet cancel control on any unfinished Task, and a Task cancellation that stops its Worker before it answers (D78 revision of 2026-11-13; renderer plus one RPC handler)

Planner brief, 2026-11-13 (verifier). M3-09-2b-3 put `Cancel Task` on the Task page where a pending item of the Task lists the cancellation — a Task that is blocked, under review, flagged as stalled, or behind a canceled upstream Task. A Task that needs nobody — running, waiting for a dependency, for admission, for verification or for the integration queue — has no cancel control at all. Henry decided on 2026-11-13 (D78, revision row): every unfinished Task gets one, quiet, on its Task page. This part adds it, and closes the gap that matters most for the new case: today the Worker of a canceled Task keeps running until the next reconciliation tick, up to a minute later, and the page shows a canceled Task with a running Attempt until someone refreshes.

Read first: `AGENTS.md` (UI and Product Experience; Destructive Actions), `docs/STYLEGUIDE.md` (Buttons; UX rules 3 and 4), `docs/decisions/DECISION_LOG.md` D78 with its revision of 2026-11-13, `docs/reference/control-plane.md` ("Task 页的调整与取消（M3-09-2b-3）", and the paragraph that describes `axiom.mission.cancel`).

## Ground truth (verifier, 2026-11-13, main `6eca7fa4f4`; every fact here was read in the code, the tests or the specs at that commit)

1. **Cancel on the page today.** `TaskVerificationActions.tsx` (region "Task actions") offers `Cancel Task` only when a pending item of the Task lists `axiom.task.cancel`, next to the three other actions, in item order; it renders the button whether or not the page passes a handler. `TaskCancelForm.tsx` takes that `item`: the consequence sentence (`missions.cancelTaskConsequence`), then — for a blocking item with `blocks` above 1 — "Tasks that depend on it: N. They wait until a person cancels them too." with N = `blocks − 1`, for an item that is not blocking the sentence without a number (`missions.cancelTaskDependentsUnknown`), otherwise nothing; a required rationale; a destructive `Cancel Task` and a ghost `Close`. It calls `onSubmit(params, consequence)`; the page controller's `cancelTask` shows the confirmation dialog (title and confirm `Cancel Task`, dismiss `Keep Task`) and only then calls `axiom.task.cancel` through `mutate`, which re-reads the page after every call, accepted or refused. `MissionsPageSurface.tsx` always passes `onCancelTask`.
2. **The region is absent when no pending item offers an action**, and that is pinned: six expectations in `TaskAdjustmentActions.test.tsx`, `TaskEvidenceRead.test.tsx` and `TaskReviewActions.test.tsx`, and five in `mission-review-actions.spec.ts` and `mission-task-actions.spec.ts` (`toHaveCount(0)` on the region). Where the region is present, tests and specs pin the exact list of its buttons. When nothing is offered, the pending read failed and the Task waits on a person, the component shows the note `missions.taskActionsReadError` in the region's place. One existing test opens the region's cancel form, re-renders without the item and expects the region gone and focus on the Task detail section (`TaskAdjustmentActions.test.tsx`, "offers adjust and cancel in the order the pending item lists them"); it passes `onCancelTask`, so after this Task the quiet control appears at that point — it must not take the focus.
3. **What may be canceled.** `src/shared/axiom-contract/lifecycle.ts`: `TASK_STAGE_TRANSITIONS` lets every stage except `INTEGRATED` and `CANCELED` move to `CANCELED`; `isLegalTaskStageTransition(from, to)` is what the controller applies, and `cancelTask` adds no condition about the Mission. So a Task of a plan that is not approved yet, a paused Mission's Task and a Task in the integration queue can all be canceled. The renderer already reads the Mission's table the same way (`MissionActions.tsx`, `isLegalMissionTransition(status, 'CANCELED')` for `Cancel Mission`); it does not use the Task function yet.
4. **What a cancellation does.** `MissionController.cancelTask` refuses a rationale that looks like a secret before its transaction, then moves the Task to `CANCELED`, resolves its pending `BLOCKED` messages, and asks for a scheduling round. It stops nothing: "Cancellation commits intent first; RecoveryService stops/releases Attempts after commit" (`task-readiness.ts`). `reconcileAttempt` (`recovery/attempt-reconciliation.ts`) sees a canceled Task or a terminal Mission and calls `cancelAttempt` (`recovery/attempt-recovery-effects.ts`): a Worker that is provably live is stopped through the kernel and released, the audit `attempt.stopped` is written and the Attempt reads `STOPPED`; one that cannot be proven live is abandoned (`attempt.abandoned`); a stop the kernel cannot confirm is recorded as `attempt.stop_unknown` and left to the next pass; a submitted Attempt is never stopped. All three audits carry `reason: 'mission_canceled'`, also when only the Task was canceled.
5. **When reconciliation runs.** At boot, every 60 seconds (`ATTEMPT_RECONCILE_INTERVAL_MS`, `control-plane-boot.ts`), after a mailbox escalation (`control-plane.ts`), and inside `axiom.mission.cancel`: that handler (`src/main/runtime/rpc/methods/axiom/mission.ts`) calls `cancelMission` and then awaits `plane.recovery.reconcileAttempts({ missionId })` before it answers. `axiom.task.cancel` (`src/main/runtime/rpc/methods/axiom/task.ts`) only calls `cancelTask`. `RecoveryService.reconcileAttempts` is single-flight (a call that arrives during a pass waits for it and then runs its own), takes a Mission filter, and catches the failure of one Attempt without failing the pass.
6. **Dependents.** A cancellation does not cascade (DEF-21 ①): a Task that depends on a canceled one waits on `dependency · upstream_canceled` with `person: true`, and the pending view gives it a `task_blocked` item that lists only `axiom.task.cancel` — so its page offers `Cancel Task` in the region.
7. **Tests that already cover the neighbourhood.** `src/main/axiom/recovery/attempt-reconciliation.test.ts` has the Mission counterpart ("cancels live and unreachable workers through the kernel and records release dispositions"): it calls `axiom.mission.cancel` through the dispatcher on `channelFixture()` (`execution/attempt-channel-fixture.test-support.ts`), whose Attempts are bound to a runtime the test spies on; the file's setup already carries the fake clock those tests need. `rpc-task-cancel-rationale.test.ts`, `rpc-reads.test.ts` and `read-models/waiting-on-stages.test.ts` call `axiom.task.cancel` through the dispatcher and must keep passing unchanged.
8. **E2E harness.** `withFixtureMission(…, { taskIds: ['T01', 'T04'], holdWorkers: true, … })` keeps the Worker of T01 running (`waitingOn` `running · attempt_running`) until `releaseWorkers()` removes the hold file; T04 waits on `dependency · upstream_not_integrated`. `axiom.attempt.list` gives each Attempt's `state`; the Task page lists it in the table `Attempts` (`Running`, `Stopped`, …). `captureDetail` (`tests/e2e/helpers/axiom-task-detail-capture.ts`) attaches a full-height screenshot of the Task detail. The third scenario of `mission-task-actions.spec.ts` cancels a Task under review through the region, dismissing the dialog with `Keep Task` once before confirming; its helpers (`openDetail`, `readTask`) are local to that spec.

## Outcome

On the page of any Task that is not finished, a person can cancel it: where a pending item already offers the cancellation, exactly as today; everywhere else, through one quiet button that opens the same form and the same confirmation. When the call answers, the Worker of the canceled Task has been stopped, and the page's one re-read shows the Task canceled and its Attempt stopped. Nothing else about cancellation changes: it does not cascade, the Control Plane decides whether it is legal, and a refusal is shown as it comes.

## Deliverables

1. **A quiet `Cancel Task` on the Task page.** In the Task detail, outside the region "Task actions", a button named `Cancel Task` is shown when all three hold: the Task's stage may still move to `CANCELED` by the shared table (`isLegalTaskStageTransition(task.stage, 'CANCELED')` — read from `lifecycle.ts`, not repeated), no pending item of this Task lists `axiom.task.cancel` (then the region offers it, as today), and the page passes `onCancelTask`. So a Task page has at any time exactly one control that opens the cancel form, and a finished Task has none.
   - Quiet: ghost variant, small, placed after the waiting line and the region's place in the header block of the Task detail; no colour, badge or heading of its own. It is not a destructive button — the destructive one is inside the form, as today.
   - It opens `TaskCancelForm` in place (the button carries `aria-expanded`, and `aria-controls` while the form is open, like the buttons of the region); focus moves to the rationale. `Close` hides the form and returns focus to the quiet button. Submitting goes through the page's existing `onCancelTask(params, consequence)` — the same confirmation dialog, `Keep Task` leaving everything as it was, and the same `mutate`. Nothing is called when the form is only opened or closed. The form belongs to the Task it was opened on: re-rendered for another Task, the control shows no open form and no typed text (the page already remounts the Task detail per Task; the control must not depend on that).
   - When the control goes away while its form is open — the re-read shows the Task as `CANCELED`, or a pending item of the Task now lists the cancellation, so the region offers it — the form goes with it and focus moves to the Task detail section. When the control appears (a re-read removed the pending item that listed the cancellation), it does not take the focus.
   - After a refusal the form stays with what was typed and the refusal is shown verbatim in the page alert, as for every other action.
   - It is disabled while the page is busy.
   - A failed pending read does not hide it: when the region offers nothing — there is no item, or the read failed — and the stage allows it, the quiet button is there (below the note of ground truth 2 when that note is shown).
2. **The form without a pending item.** `TaskCancelForm`'s `item` becomes optional. Without an item it shows the consequence sentence and the sentence without a number (`missions.cancelTaskDependentsUnknown`) — the page does not know how many Tasks depend on this one and does not work it out. With an item it behaves exactly as today.
3. **`axiom.task.cancel` stops the Worker before it answers** (main process, one RPC handler). After `cancelTask` has returned — the cancellation is committed — the handler awaits the Attempt reconciliation of that Mission, the same call `axiom.mission.cancel` makes (`plane.recovery.reconcileAttempts({ missionId })`), and only then answers. The response does not change (`{ task }`); no parameter, schema, authority rule or CLI output changes. A cancellation that is refused reconciles nothing. A reconciliation that throws is logged the way the Control Plane already logs it and does not turn the committed cancellation into an error: the call still answers with the canceled Task, and the timer repeats the reconciliation. Nothing else changes: no new stop path, no new audit event or reason, nothing in `MissionController`, `RecoveryService` or `control-plane.ts`.
4. **Catalogs.** No new string is needed (`cancelTask`, the form's keys and `close` exist in all seven catalogs). If you do add one, it goes through `translate('missions.…', 'English')` into all seven catalogs (`en` and `zh` with real text) and is listed in the report.
5. **E2E** (host, verifier-run): a new spec `tests/e2e/mission-task-cancel.spec.ts`, every person action through the page, `desktopCall` only for setup and for reading state back, responses parsed with the contract schemas, helpers it needs defined in the spec itself; every wait is an assertion with a timeout, never a sleep.
   - **A person cancels a running Task** (the harness of ground truth 8, Workers held, no verification needed). T01 is `RUNNING` on `running · attempt_running` with one Attempt whose state is `RUNNING`. T04's page (`dependency · upstream_not_integrated`) has no region "Task actions" and exactly one button named `Cancel Task` in the Task detail. T01's page: the same; the table `Attempts` shows its Attempt as `Running`. On T01 the button opens the form `Cancel Task` with the consequence sentence and the sentence without a number, focus in `Rationale`; a rationale is typed; the form's `Cancel Task` opens the dialog `Cancel Task` with both sentences; `Keep Task` → the dialog is gone, T01 is still `RUNNING`, no audit was written, the form still holds the rationale; again, and `Cancel Task` in the dialog → the page shows the Task as canceled and, without any refresh by the test, its Attempt as `Stopped`; the detail has no button named `Cancel Task` and no form. Read back: T01's stage is `CANCELED` with the rationale as the reason of its last `task.stage` audit; its Attempt's state is `STOPPED`; exactly one `attempt.stopped` audit exists for it; the Workers were not released before this point. Then T04: it waits on `dependency · upstream_canceled`, its region "Task actions" offers exactly `Cancel Task`, and with the form closed the detail has exactly one button with that name. `releaseWorkers()` is called at the end so the harness can shut down.
   - The spec attaches two screenshots of the Task detail (`captureDetail`): T01 with the quiet button before the form is opened, and T01 with the form open and the rationale typed.
   - The report lists every asserted selector and field with its source.
6. **Docs.** `docs/reference/control-plane.md`: a short section "Task 页的安静取消入口（M3-09-2b-4）" — when the quiet button is shown and from which rule, that the region keeps the cancellation when a pending item lists it, the form without a number — and, where the document describes `axiom.mission.cancel`, that `axiom.task.cancel` now also awaits the reconciliation of its Mission after the cancellation is committed, with its response unchanged and a failed reconciliation left to the timer. `docs/GLOSSARY.md` is not changed.

## Non-goals

Cascading a cancellation or refusing one that has dependents (DEF-21 ①); a cancel control on Mission Home or in the Task table; bulk cancel; stopping an Attempt without canceling its Task; counting dependents in the page; any rule in the page about when canceling is wise (a plan under review, a Task in the integration queue — the Control Plane decides); any change to what the pending view lists or to the region; any change to what reconciliation does once it runs, including the reason its audits give; the timed refresh and times on Mission Home (M3-09-3b); removing `pendingMessages` (M3-09-3b); the copy pass (M3-10).
