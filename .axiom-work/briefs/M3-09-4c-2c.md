# Task M3-09-4c-2c — the Mission page shows who plans and who works, which providers a Mission may use and why each one can or cannot be used now, lets a person change the Planner provider, the Worker provider, the allowed providers and "only certified combinations", and opens that editor from the pending item that says no provider is eligible (the second renderer half of the policy editor; renderer only: no main-process, contract or CLI change)

Planner brief, 2026-11-24 (verifier; first draft by an Opus subagent from the verifier's design and a sketch of the product files, then checked against the code and finished by the verifier). A Mission's policy decides which provider plans and which providers work, which providers are not allowed, and whether only certified combinations may run. When none of the providers can take a Task, the Task waits, and Mission Home says so in a pending item — "You can: Update policy, Cancel Task" — but nothing on the page lets a person see why each provider is refused or change the part of the policy that refuses it: the write accepts only the desktop application, and the application has no control for it. M3-09-4c-2b built the first half of the editor (the **Policy** section: how many Tasks run at once and what verification runs). This Task builds the second half: a separate **Providers** section directly after Policy that shows the stored choices, and, for a Mission that is not finished, a form in place that changes them and shows — read once when the form opens — which providers the Control Plane would accept now and for what reason the others are refused; the pending item that offers `axiom.policy.update` gets a button that opens this form. It is **not**: a model or effort selector (whether a role's model is bound to its provider is a question that is open with Henry, and effort goes with the model), an editor of `preferredProviders`, `providerConcurrency`, `models`, `highRiskTier` or `crossProvider` (carried through untouched and not shown), a preview of availability for the draft, availability in read mode, or any main-process, contract or CLI change. The Worker changes renderer files, the two locale files, one new e2e spec and one reference document only.

Read first: `docs/PROJECT_CONSTITUTION.md`, `AGENTS.md` (UI and Product Experience; Lazy Senior Dev Mode), `docs/STYLEGUIDE.md` (the table of controls — `Select` for a single choice from a known list, line 174 — and Form anatomy), `docs/reference/control-plane.md` (the subsections `### Provider selection（M2-03）`, `### Policy 的写入与 provider 可用性（M3-09-4c-2a）` and `### Mission 页的 Policy 块与编辑（M3-09-4c-2b）`), `.axiom-work/briefs/M3-09-4c-2b.md`, `docs/reference/reuse-map.md` (the row `M3-09-4c-2 policy 编辑`: the model catalog and the selector it names are NOT used in this Task — no model or effort control; what this Task reuses is this folder's own — `MissionPolicy.tsx` with `MissionPolicyForm.tsx` and `policy-draft.ts` for a policy setting shown and changed in place, `policy-actions.ts` for the confirmation, `TaskDetail.tsx` for moving focus to a part of the page — the primitives `Button`, `Label`, `Checkbox`, `Select`, and `getAgentLabel` from `src/renderer/src/lib/agent-catalog.tsx` for provider names).

## Outcome

The Mission page has a **Providers** section directly after Policy. It shows who plans (`Automatic` or a provider's name), who works (the same), which providers are not allowed (names, or `None`) and whether only certified combinations may run (`Yes` / `No`); a role's model or effort stored by another caller is shown after the role as text. For a Mission that is not in a terminal status a person can open a form in place — from the section's button or from the button of the pending item that offers `axiom.policy.update` — and the page then reads, once, which providers the Control Plane would accept now under the saved policy, and shows it as a table: per provider, whether it can plan and whether it can work Tasks of low, medium and high risk, or the reason it cannot, in words. The form changes the Planner provider, the Worker provider, the allowed providers and "only certified combinations"; saving asks first through the same confirmation as the Policy section, naming what changes and when it applies, and writes only after the person confirmed; it sends the routing stored now with only the changed keys replaced. The section then shows what the page's next read answers; the admission round that every committed update requests can admit the waiting Task, and the pending item goes away at a later read.
