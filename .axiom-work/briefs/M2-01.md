## Objective

Give the Control Plane (M2-05 attempt-launcher, M2-09 Planner) a trusted, in-process way to start a Worker on a Mission Run without pretending to be a terminal: one optional, service-only argument bag on `startLocalWorker` that (a) sets the dispatch creator to `{ kind: 'system' }`, (b) targets a precreated Attempt worktree by id, (c) names the branch when the kernel creates the worktree, (d) replaces the preamble text with a Control Plane builder while keeping delivery and turn observation, (e) caps the dispatch depth, and (f) records lineage without treating a session address as a terminal. **Absent the bag, every existing caller behaves byte-for-byte as today**; the RPC `orchestration.workerStart` schema and handler do not change (and M1-07 keeps refusing it on Mission Runs, D10).
