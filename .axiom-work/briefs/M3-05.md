## Outcome

After this Task: every INCONCLUSIVE verdict becomes a `HUMAN_REVIEW` run the human can see in `axiom verification list --status HUMAN_REVIEW`; the human resolves it with `axiom.verification.resolve` to PASS (Task `VERIFIED`) or FAIL with a rationale and the criteria they judged failed (Task `NEEDS_FIX` → retry or `BLOCKED`, exactly as a deterministic FAIL), and that rationale is rendered, quoted, into the next Attempt's context; a deterministic `PASS` / `FAIL` run cannot be resolved at all (exit 3); when the plan — not the candidate — was wrong (`plan_invalid`, `candidate_disputed`, a wrongly written human criterion), the human replaces the Task's acceptance criteria with `axiom.task.adjust` and asks for re-verification of the same candidate with `axiom.verification.rerun`; nothing else changes (no Reviewer, no automatic answer, no new table or stage).
