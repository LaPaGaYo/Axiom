## Outcome

After this Task: a proposal (or a desktop-created Task, a REQUEST_SUBTASK, a criteria replacement) whose Task has no `command` or `artifact` criterion is rejected in the same turn with `plan_invalid { errors: [{ code: 'acceptance_uncheckable', … }] }` and the Planner contract says so; a proposal that leaves Goal acceptance criteria uncovered or lets two parallel Tasks write the same paths is **accepted with warnings** — the warnings are persisted on the PlanRevision, returned by `axiom.plan.show / list`, printed by `axiom plan show`, echoed in the `plan_propose` MCP result, and carried in the `plan.approved` audit so the approval is evidence that a human saw them; `axiom.plan.show` also returns the coverage matrix (Goal criterion → Tasks) that the M4 completion gate (DEF-20 ②) will read. Nothing is rejected that the frozen fixture produces.
