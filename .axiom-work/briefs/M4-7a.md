# Task M4-7a — a Mission that is ready asks for its delivery Approval: one PENDING row bound to the report it was made for

**Why.** Since M4-10 a finished Mission reaches `READY_FOR_APPROVAL` with a completion report whose id and hash the finalization record names — and then nothing can move it on. V1 has exactly one approval, the delivery approval (D92 a): a person approves delivering *these* commits, and the approval dies when they change (D70 item 2). The table `approvals` has existed since M1 with no writer and no reader of a row. This Task gives the Approval its contract, its store and its lifecycle, and makes the Control Plane request one — in the transaction that makes the Mission ready — bound to the hash of the report, which already holds the Mission, the target branch, both commits, every Task and the gate. **Nobody can grant or deny yet** (M4-7b adds the two desktop-only methods), nothing expires yet, and nothing is delivered (M4-8 consumes a granted Approval). The page that shows the request is M4-11; until then a waiting Mission shows one pending item whose only action is cancel.

High-risk class: it creates the object that will authorize delivery, with a lifecycle of its own.

## Outcome

When finalization records `ready`, the same transaction requests one delivery Approval: a `PENDING` row of kind `delivery` whose `inputsHash` is the report's hash, that expires 72 hours later, with an audit event. Canceling a Mission revokes its open delivery Approvals in the cancel's transaction; pausing keeps them. `axiom.mission.show` carries the Mission's latest delivery Approval. A Mission that waits shows one blocking pending item, `delivery_approval_pending`, that names the target branch, both commits, the report and the expiry, and offers only cancel. No method grants, denies or lists; nothing expires; nothing is delivered.
